KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

Common signs of gum disease

Healthy gums generally do not bleed regularly during routine brushing or flossing. Gum inflammation may begin subtly, so recognising early changes can help you seek care before the problem progresses.

Bleeding gums

Bleeding while brushing, flossing or eating can be a sign of gum inflammation and should be assessed if it persists.

Red or swollen gums

Gums that look red, puffy, tender or feel different from usual may indicate inflammation.

Persistent bad breath

Plaque, calculus and gum infection can contribute to ongoing mouth odour, although other causes are also possible.

Gum recession

Receding gums can expose root surfaces, affect appearance and contribute to sensitivity.

Loose or shifting teeth

Mobility or changes in tooth position can occur in advanced periodontal problems and need prompt assessment.

Pus or gum swelling

A localised swelling, pus or significant tenderness may indicate an infection requiring timely dental care.

WHY IT HAPPENS

What causes bleeding gums and gum disease?

  • Plaque and calculus accumulation around teeth and the gumline
  • Inadequate or inconsistent brushing and interdental cleaning
  • Smoking or tobacco use
  • Crowded teeth or areas that are difficult to clean
  • Some hormonal, systemic or medication-related factors
  • Long-standing untreated gum inflammation

Bleeding is a signal, not a cleaning technique

Some people brush harder when their gums bleed. Excessive force can irritate the gums further. Gentle, thorough cleaning and professional assessment are more useful than aggressive brushing.

If gum bleeding is frequent, spontaneous, heavy, or accompanied by swelling, pain, pus or loose teeth, arrange a dental evaluation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about gum treatment

Why do my gums bleed when I brush?

Bleeding during brushing can be associated with plaque-related gum inflammation, gingivitis, gum disease or other factors. Persistent bleeding should be assessed rather than treated as normal.

Can gum disease be cured?

Early gum inflammation can often be improved with professional cleaning and effective home care. More advanced periodontal disease may require deeper cleaning and ongoing periodontal maintenance depending on the findings.

Does scaling damage teeth?

Professional scaling is used to remove plaque and calculus deposits that cannot be removed adequately by routine brushing. Temporary sensitivity can occur in some patients, particularly when deposits have covered exposed root surfaces.

Can gum disease cause bad breath?

Yes. Plaque, calculus and gum infection can contribute to persistent bad breath, although bad breath can also have other oral or medical causes.

How much does gum treatment cost in Pune?

Cost depends on the condition and treatment required. A consultation may be followed by professional cleaning, periodontal treatment or maintenance according to the severity of gum disease.

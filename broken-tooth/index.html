FIRST STEPS

What to do after a tooth breaks

Protect the tooth

Avoid hard or sticky foods and chew on the opposite side until the tooth has been examined.

Rinse gently

Use clean water to remove debris. Avoid repeatedly poking or testing the broken area with your tongue or fingers.

Save a broken fragment

If a piece has come off, keep it clean and bring it to the dental appointment. Do not attempt to glue it back yourself.

HOW SERIOUS IS IT?

Different types of tooth damage need different treatment

  • Small chip: may be smoothed, bonded or restored with a tooth-coloured material.
  • Larger fracture: may require a filling or crown to restore strength and function.
  • Crack extending deeper: the tooth may need further assessment, sometimes including root canal treatment.
  • Trauma with a loose or displaced tooth: prompt dental evaluation is important.
  • Tooth that cannot be predictably restored: extraction may sometimes be considered, followed by discussion of replacement options.

Seek urgent care for red flags

Get prompt dental or medical help for significant facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, severe trauma, fever with dental swelling, or a tooth injury accompanied by difficulty breathing or swallowing.

For major facial trauma or breathing/swallowing difficulty, seek emergency medical care.

ASSESSMENT

How a broken tooth is evaluated

1. Examine the fracture

The dentist checks how much tooth structure is missing and whether the bite, gums and surrounding teeth are affected.

2. Check the pulp

Pain, sensitivity and clinical findings help determine whether the nerve or pulp may have been affected.

3. Consider imaging

Dental X-rays or other appropriate imaging may be recommended when the injury or symptoms suggest deeper damage.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about broken teeth

What should I do if I break a tooth?

Rinse gently with water, avoid chewing on the affected side and arrange a dental assessment. If a piece of tooth has broken off, keep it clean and bring it to the dentist if possible.

Can a broken tooth be repaired?

Often yes. Depending on how much tooth is lost and whether the pulp is affected, options can include bonding or a filling, a crown, root canal treatment followed by restoration, or extraction when the tooth cannot be predictably restored.

Is a cracked tooth a dental emergency?

A crack with severe pain, swelling, bleeding, trauma or a large piece of tooth missing should be assessed promptly. Facial swelling or difficulty breathing or swallowing requires urgent medical attention.

How much does broken tooth treatment cost in Pune?

Cost depends on the extent of damage and the treatment required, such as bonding, filling, crown, root canal treatment or extraction. An examination is needed for an individualized estimate.

Toothache: When Should You See a Dentist?

Tooth pain can have many causes, including decay, inflammation, cracks, gum problems or wisdom teeth. Persistent or severe pain deserves professional assessment.

Important: Educational content cannot replace a clinical examination. If you have persistent pain, swelling, bleeding, trauma or another concerning symptom, arrange an appropriate dental assessment.

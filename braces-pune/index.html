Who May Benefit From Braces?

Orthodontic treatment can help improve the position of teeth and, when indicated, the relationship between the upper and lower teeth. An assessment is needed to determine the most suitable approach.

Crowded Teeth

Limited space can make teeth overlap or become difficult to clean.

Gaps Between Teeth

Orthodontic planning can address spaces where treatment is appropriate.

Crooked Teeth

Braces can gradually move teeth toward a planned alignment.

Bite Problems

Overbite, underbite and crossbite may require orthodontic assessment.

What Happens During Orthodontic Treatment?

  1. Consultation: We understand your concerns and assess your teeth and bite.
  2. Records & diagnosis: Appropriate photographs, scans or radiographs may be recommended based on the case.
  3. Treatment planning: Options, expected stages and practical considerations are discussed.
  4. Active treatment: Braces or aligners are monitored with scheduled reviews and adjustments.
  5. Retention: Retainers are generally used after active tooth movement to help maintain the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best age for braces?

There is no single age that suits everyone. Children and teenagers may be assessed while the teeth and jaws are developing, while adults can also receive orthodontic treatment when clinically suitable.

Are braces painful?

Some pressure, tenderness or discomfort can occur after braces are fitted or adjusted. Your orthodontic team can advise you on managing temporary discomfort.

Braces or clear aligners—which is better?

It depends on the diagnosis, tooth movement required, lifestyle and treatment goals. Some cases are suitable for either option, while others may be better managed with fixed appliances.

Do you treat adults?

Yes. Adults can be assessed for orthodontic treatment, provided their teeth and supporting tissues are suitable for treatment.

A Straighter Smile, With a More Discreet Approach

Clear aligners are a series of removable, transparent orthodontic trays designed to move teeth gradually. Whether clear aligners are suitable depends on your teeth, bite, gum health, treatment goals and the complexity of the movement required.

Nearly Invisible

Clear trays can be less noticeable than conventional braces, making them appealing to many adults and teenagers.

Removable

Aligners can generally be removed for eating, drinking and routine oral hygiene, according to your orthodontic plan.

Planned Movement

Treatment is planned around the movements needed to improve alignment and, where appropriate, aspects of your bite.

Invisalign® at Amayra

Invisalign® is a branded clear-aligner system. It is one option within the broader category of clear aligner treatment. At consultation, the appropriate approach can be discussed based on your clinical needs rather than brand alone.

What is Invisalign®?

Invisalign® uses a sequence of custom-made clear aligners to guide planned tooth movement.

Is Invisalign® right for me?

Suitability varies. An orthodontic examination is needed to assess alignment, bite, tooth and gum health, and the movements required.

Brand vs Treatment

We can discuss Invisalign® alongside other appropriate clear-aligner options when planning your treatment.

Who May Benefit From Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners can be considered for a range of alignment concerns. Your dentist or orthodontic clinician will determine whether aligners are appropriate for your specific case.

Crowded Teeth

Limited space can cause teeth to overlap or rotate. Selected cases can be treated with aligners.

Spacing & Gaps

Aligners may help close or reduce certain spaces between teeth.

Selected Bite Concerns

Some bite problems can be managed with aligners; more complex cases may require another approach or additional treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are clear aligners better than braces?Neither is universally better. The right option depends on the diagnosis, movements required, lifestyle and treatment goals.
Is Invisalign® the same as clear aligners?Invisalign® is a specific branded clear-aligner system. “Clear aligners” is the broader category that includes different systems.
Can I eat while wearing clear aligners?Aligners are generally removed for meals. Follow the specific instructions provided for your treatment.
How long does clear-aligner treatment take?Duration varies significantly by case complexity and the planned movements. Your clinician can provide an individualized estimate after assessment.
Can teenagers have clear aligners?Some teenagers are suitable candidates. Suitability depends on dental development, orthodontic needs and the ability to follow the prescribed wear routine.
Do I need retainers after aligner treatment?Retention is generally an important part of orthodontic treatment because teeth can shift over time.

WHAT AFFECTS PRICE?

Why root canal treatment costs vary

There is no single RCT price that applies to every tooth. A dentist first needs to determine whether the pulp is inflamed or infected, whether the tooth can be predictably restored, and how complex the root canal anatomy is.

Type of tooth

Front teeth, premolars and molars can have different canal numbers and anatomy, affecting treatment time and complexity.

Number & shape of canals

Additional or unusually shaped canals may require more detailed cleaning, shaping and treatment planning.

Infection & inflammation

The extent of infection and the condition of surrounding tissues can influence the clinical plan and number of visits.

Retreatment

A previously treated tooth may need more complex management than an initial root canal.

Imaging

Appropriate dental X-rays or other imaging may be required to assess anatomy and plan treatment.

Final restoration

The tooth may need a filling, crown or another restoration after RCT depending on remaining tooth structure and function.

UNDERSTAND THE COMPLETE PLAN

RCT cost is not always the complete treatment cost

When comparing quotes, check whether the estimate refers only to the root canal procedure or also includes consultation, imaging, temporary restoration and the final crown or restoration.

Diagnosis & imaging

The assessment identifies whether RCT is appropriate and whether additional dental problems need treatment.

Root canal procedure

The diseased pulp is removed, the canal system is cleaned and shaped, and the canals are sealed.

Core restoration

After RCT, the tooth needs a suitable restoration to restore function and protect remaining structure.

Crown when indicated

Many root-treated posterior teeth benefit from a protective crown, depending on the tooth and remaining structure.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Questions to ask about an RCT quotation

  • Is the quoted fee for the complete RCT or per visit/tooth stage?
  • Are diagnostic X-rays or other indicated imaging included?
  • Is a temporary restoration included?
  • Is the final crown or restoration quoted separately?
  • Does the tooth need retreatment or another procedure?
  • What follow-up is expected?

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about RCT cost

How much does a root canal cost in Pune?

The cost varies with tooth type, canal anatomy, infection or retreatment complexity, imaging, visits and the final restoration. An examination is needed for an individual estimate.

Is a crown included in the root canal cost?

Not necessarily. A crown may be recommended after RCT, particularly for many back teeth, and it may be quoted separately.

Does a molar RCT cost more than a front-tooth RCT?

It can, because molars often have more complex canal anatomy and may require more treatment time. The exact plan depends on the tooth.

Does retreatment cost more?

Retreatment can be more complex because previous filling materials may need to be managed and the canal system reassessed.

Can I get an exact RCT price online?

For an exact estimate, the tooth should be examined and relevant imaging reviewed when indicated. Online guides cannot replace a clinical assessment.

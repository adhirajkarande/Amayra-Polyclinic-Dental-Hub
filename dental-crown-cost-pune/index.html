WHY PRICES VARY

What determines dental crown cost?

A crown is a custom restoration, so its cost cannot be judged from the material name alone. The condition of the tooth and the work required to make it suitable for restoration are also important.

Crown material

Different materials have different aesthetic, functional and laboratory characteristics. The best option depends on the tooth and clinical requirements.

Condition of the tooth

More extensive damage may require additional preparation, a core build-up or other restorative steps before the crown.

Front vs back tooth

Tooth position, biting forces and aesthetic requirements can influence restoration planning and material selection.

Laboratory work

Custom fabrication and laboratory processes are part of the final restoration and can influence the estimate.

Root canal treatment

If the tooth needs root canal treatment, that procedure is generally considered separately from the final crown.

Additional treatment

Gum treatment, replacement of an old restoration or other preparatory procedures may sometimes be needed.

COMPARE QUOTATIONS FAIRLY

What should a crown estimate include?

  • Clinical examination and treatment planning
  • Relevant dental imaging when indicated
  • Tooth preparation and temporary restoration where required
  • Chosen crown material and laboratory fabrication
  • Try-in, cementation/fitting and bite adjustment
  • Planned follow-up and review

Ask for an itemised estimate

When comparing crown prices, ask exactly what is included. A lower headline price may exclude preparation, temporary restoration, laboratory charges or other necessary treatment.

CROWN OR ANOTHER OPTION?

Diagnosis comes before the price

A crown may be appropriate for a substantially weakened, fractured or heavily restored tooth, but not every damaged tooth needs a crown. Depending on the amount of remaining tooth structure, alternatives can include a filling, onlay or another restoration.

After root canal

Some root-treated teeth may need a protective crown depending on the tooth type and remaining structure.

Dental crown cost FAQs

How much does a dental crown cost in Pune?

There is no single price for every case. The estimate depends on the crown material, tooth condition, laboratory work and any additional treatment required.

Does the crown price include root canal treatment?

Not necessarily. Root canal treatment, core build-up and the final crown can be separate parts of a treatment plan.

Which crown material is best?

There is no universal best material. The choice depends on the tooth, bite, aesthetic requirements, remaining tooth structure and clinical recommendation.

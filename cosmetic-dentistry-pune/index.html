FAQ

Cosmetic Dentistry FAQs

What is cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry includes dental treatments intended to improve aspects of the appearance of teeth or the smile. Some treatments also restore tooth function.

What cosmetic dental treatments are available?

Depending on individual needs, options may include whitening, tooth-coloured restorations, veneers, crowns, orthodontic treatment and smile makeover planning.

Is cosmetic dentistry suitable for everyone?

Suitability depends on oral health, the condition of the teeth and gums, bite, expectations and the specific treatment being considered.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost in Pune?

There is no single price because cosmetic dentistry covers several different procedures. The final cost depends on the treatment plan, materials and complexity.

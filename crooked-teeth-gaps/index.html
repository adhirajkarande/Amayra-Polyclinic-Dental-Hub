Crowded or Crooked Teeth

Teeth that overlap, rotate or lack adequate space may be evaluated for orthodontic correction and improved cleanability.

Why Should Crooked Teeth or Gaps Be Evaluated?

  • Some alignment problems can make brushing and flossing more difficult.
  • Certain bite relationships may contribute to uneven tooth wear or functional concerns.
  • Gaps may have different causes, so closing them without identifying the cause may not be appropriate.
  • A planned orthodontic approach can focus on both tooth position and overall bite rather than appearance alone.

How Is Treatment Planned?

  1. Assessment: We review your concerns, dental history, oral health, bite and tooth positions.
  2. Records and imaging: Photographs, scans or X-rays may be recommended when clinically indicated.
  3. Options discussion: We compare suitable braces, aligner or restorative approaches, including practical considerations.
  4. Active treatment: Teeth are moved progressively with scheduled reviews and adjustments or new aligner trays.
  5. Retention: Retainers are generally important after active orthodontic treatment to help maintain the result.

FAQs About Crooked Teeth & Gaps

What causes crooked teeth or gaps between teeth?

They can be related to genetics, jaw and tooth size, early tooth loss, oral habits, tooth movement over time or other bite-related factors. A dental and orthodontic assessment helps identify the cause.

Can clear aligners fix crooked teeth?

Clear aligners can correct many mild to moderate alignment and spacing problems when a dentist or orthodontist confirms that they are suitable. More complex cases may require braces or other approaches.

Are braces better than clear aligners?

Neither is universally better. The choice depends on the type and severity of tooth movement needed, bite, age, oral health, treatment goals, compliance and clinical assessment.

Can gaps between teeth be closed without braces?

Some gaps can be managed with aligners or selected cosmetic/restorative treatments. The correct option depends on why the gap exists and the health and position of the teeth and gums.

How much does crooked teeth treatment cost in Pune?

Cost varies with the treatment method, case complexity, treatment duration and materials or laboratory requirements. An examination is needed for a personalized estimate.

Can adults get treatment for crooked teeth?

Yes. Adults can often be treated with braces or clear aligners. Suitability depends on gum health, bone support, bite and the planned tooth movements.

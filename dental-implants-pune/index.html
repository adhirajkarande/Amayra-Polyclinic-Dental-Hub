UNDERSTANDING IMPLANTS

What are dental implants?

A dental implant is a tooth-replacement option that uses an implant fixture placed in the jawbone to support a restoration such as a crown, bridge or denture. Treatment is planned after a clinical examination and appropriate diagnostic assessment.

Implants may be considered for a single missing tooth, several missing teeth or selected full-arch situations. Suitability depends on factors such as bone volume, gum health, general health, oral hygiene and the proposed restoration.

Considering an implant?

Start with an assessment rather than choosing a treatment based only on price. A proper evaluation helps determine whether an implant, bridge, denture or another option is appropriate.

THE PROCESS

How dental implant treatment works

  1. Consultation & examination: We assess your teeth, gums, bite and overall oral condition.
  2. Diagnostic planning: Appropriate imaging may be used to assess bone and plan implant placement.
  3. Treatment planning: We explain the proposed sequence, alternatives, expected timelines and fees.
  4. Implant placement: The implant is placed according to the planned surgical approach.
  5. Healing & integration: The implant is allowed to integrate with the surrounding bone as clinically appropriate.
  6. Restoration: A crown, bridge or other planned restoration is fitted after the relevant healing and stability requirements are met.

SUITABILITY

Who may be a candidate?

Many adults with missing teeth can be considered for implants, but suitability is individual. Healthy gums, adequate bone or a feasible bone-management plan, good oral hygiene and realistic expectations are important factors.

Smoking, uncontrolled systemic conditions, active gum disease and insufficient bone can affect treatment planning and outcomes. Your dentist will discuss these factors during consultation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about dental implants

Are dental implants painful?

Implant placement is performed using appropriate local anaesthesia. Some soreness, swelling or discomfort can occur after treatment and is usually managed with the post-operative instructions provided by the dental team.

How long does an implant take?

The overall timeline varies. It can include consultation, diagnostics, implant placement, healing and restoration. Your dentist can give you a more accurate timeline after assessment.

Can I get an implant immediately after tooth removal?

In selected cases, immediate implant placement may be possible. It depends on factors such as infection, bone condition, stability and the overall treatment plan.

Are dental implants suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability is individual and depends on oral health, bone, gum condition, medical factors, hygiene and other considerations. A consultation is necessary.

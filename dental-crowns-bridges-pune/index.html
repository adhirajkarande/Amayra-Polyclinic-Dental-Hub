UNDERSTAND YOUR OPTIONS

What are dental crowns and bridges?

A dental crown is a custom-made restoration that covers and protects a damaged or weakened tooth. It may also be used to restore a tooth after root canal treatment or as part of certain implant restorations.

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by using neighbouring teeth or other suitable support. The right choice depends on the number and position of missing teeth, gum health, bite, bone support and your long-term goals.

Dental Crowns

Useful when a tooth has lost substantial structure, is cracked or weakened, or needs a protective full-coverage restoration.

Dental Bridges

A fixed tooth-replacement option for selected cases where one or more adjacent teeth are missing.

Implant Crowns

A crown can be placed over a dental implant to restore the visible part of a missing tooth after appropriate implant planning.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a crown the same as a filling?

No. A filling replaces part of a tooth, while a crown covers and protects a larger portion of a tooth when more structure has been lost.

Can a crown be placed after root canal treatment?

Often, yes. Depending on the tooth and remaining structure, a crown may be recommended to help protect a root-treated tooth.

Are dental bridges permanent?

Bridges are fixed restorations, but they are not lifetime devices. Their longevity depends on oral hygiene, bite, supporting teeth and regular dental care.

Can a bridge replace a missing front tooth?

In suitable cases, yes. The dentist will assess the supporting teeth, gums, bite and aesthetic requirements before recommending a bridge or another option.

What is better: a bridge or an implant?

Neither is universally better. The appropriate option depends on your oral health, bone support, adjacent teeth, preferences, treatment goals and overall suitability.

DON'T IGNORE RECURRING PAIN

Why does my tooth hurt?

Toothache is a symptom rather than a diagnosis. Pain may be sharp, throbbing, constant, intermittent or triggered by hot, cold, sweet foods or biting. The treatment depends on what is causing the pain and how much of the tooth or surrounding tissues are affected.

Tooth decay

A cavity can cause sensitivity and pain as decay progresses deeper into the tooth.

Pulp inflammation or infection

Deep decay, trauma or cracks can affect the pulp and may require root canal treatment.

Cracked or damaged tooth

Pain on biting or releasing the bite can occur with certain cracks or fractures.

Gum problems

Inflamed or infected gums can cause tenderness, swelling and discomfort around a tooth.

Wisdom tooth problems

Partially erupted or impacted wisdom teeth can cause pain and inflammation at the back of the mouth.

Tooth sensitivity

Exposed dentine or gum recession can cause short, sharp sensitivity to temperature or sweet foods.

WHEN TO SEEK CARE

Toothache warning signs

  • Pain that lasts, returns frequently or is getting worse
  • Pain that wakes you from sleep
  • Swelling around the tooth or face
  • Fever or feeling unwell with dental pain
  • Pain on biting or chewing
  • Bad taste, pus or drainage near a tooth
  • Significant sensitivity that persists after the trigger is removed
  • Tooth pain following an injury or fracture

Get urgent help if…

Dental pain accompanied by rapidly increasing facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, major trauma, or difficulty breathing or swallowing needs urgent care.

Breathing or swallowing difficulty can be a medical emergency. Seek emergency medical care immediately rather than waiting for a routine dental appointment.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about toothache

What causes a toothache?

Common causes include tooth decay, pulp inflammation or infection, cracks or fractures, gum problems, sensitivity and wisdom tooth problems. An examination is needed to identify the specific cause.

Does toothache always mean I need a root canal?

No. A filling or other treatment may be enough in some cases. Root canal treatment is considered when the dental pulp is irreversibly inflamed or infected and the tooth is suitable to save.

Can a toothache go away on its own?

Pain can temporarily settle even when the underlying problem remains. Recurrent or persistent pain should be assessed rather than relying on pain relief alone.

When is tooth pain an emergency?

Severe or rapidly worsening pain, facial swelling, fever, uncontrolled bleeding or significant dental trauma needs urgent assessment. Difficulty breathing or swallowing requires emergency medical care.

How much does toothache treatment cost in Pune?

There is no single toothache treatment price because the cause determines the treatment. Costs can vary for fillings, root canal treatment, extraction, gum treatment and restorations. We can discuss the expected treatment and cost after examination.

What Is a Smile Makeover?

There is no single “smile makeover” procedure. Treatment is planned after examining your teeth, gums, bite and existing restorations. Depending on your needs, the plan may involve one treatment or a combination of procedures.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening can improve the shade of suitable natural teeth after assessment.

Veneers

Veneers may be considered for selected changes in tooth shape, colour or proportions.

Crowns

When teeth need greater structural restoration, crowns may combine function with aesthetic goals.

Clear Aligners

For selected alignment concerns, orthodontic treatment can improve tooth position before or as part of a smile plan.

Tooth-Coloured Restorations

Conservative restorative options can improve the appearance of teeth affected by decay, wear or old restorations.

Gum & Bite Assessment

A healthy gum foundation and appropriate bite are important before purely cosmetic treatment is considered.

Who May Consider a Smile Makeover?

Patients may seek cosmetic dental care for a variety of concerns. The right treatment depends on the cause of the concern and the health of the teeth and gums.

Discoloured Teeth

Staining or unwanted tooth colour may sometimes be improved with whitening or restorative options.

Uneven or Worn Teeth

Changes in shape or wear may be addressed with appropriately selected restorative or cosmetic procedures.

Gaps or Crowding

Orthodontic assessment can determine whether aligners, braces or another approach is appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a smile makeover one procedure?No. It is a personalized treatment plan that can combine different cosmetic and restorative procedures.
Can I get a smile makeover if I have cavities or gum disease?Existing dental disease generally needs to be assessed and managed before elective cosmetic treatment is planned.
Does a smile makeover include veneers?Veneers can be one component of a smile makeover when they are clinically appropriate, but they are not required for every patient.
Can clear aligners be part of a smile makeover?Yes, selected patients may benefit from orthodontic alignment as part of an overall smile plan.
How long does a smile makeover take?Timing depends on the treatments involved and whether procedures need to be completed in stages.

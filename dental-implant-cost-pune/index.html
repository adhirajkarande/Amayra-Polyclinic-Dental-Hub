THE TOTAL COST IS CASE-SPECIFIC

What makes dental implant cost different from patient to patient?

A dental implant is not just one item on a bill. Treatment commonly involves assessment, the implant fixture, an abutment and a final tooth replacement, with other procedures added only when clinically indicated.

Number of implants

Replacing one missing tooth is different from replacing several teeth or supporting a larger restoration.

Implant system

Implant systems can differ in design, components, clinical indications and laboratory workflow.

Abutment & crown

The restorative components placed over the implant can influence the overall treatment estimate.

Bone condition

If there is insufficient bone for the planned implant, additional procedures may be considered after assessment.

Imaging & planning

Clinical examination and appropriate dental imaging may be required to evaluate bone, neighbouring structures and implant position.

Additional treatment

Extractions, gum treatment or other preparatory procedures may sometimes be needed before implant placement.

UNDERSTAND THE COMPONENTS

What may be included in an implant treatment estimate?

  • Dental examination and treatment planning
  • Relevant dental imaging where indicated
  • Implant fixture placement
  • Abutment or restorative component
  • Final crown or other implant-supported restoration
  • Follow-up and restorative appointments as planned

Ask for an itemised estimate

When comparing implant prices, ask whether the quoted amount includes the implant, abutment, crown, imaging, laboratory charges and any additional procedures. This makes comparisons much more meaningful than comparing a single headline number.

FAQ

Dental implant cost FAQs

How much does a dental implant cost in Pune?

There is no single price for every patient. The estimate depends on the number of teeth, implant system, crown and abutment, bone condition, imaging and any additional procedures required.

Does implant cost include the crown?

Not always. Implant treatment can have separate components such as the implant fixture, abutment and final crown. Ask for an itemised estimate.

Is bone grafting included?

Bone grafting or other augmentation is considered only when clinically indicated and may be an additional procedure.

Can I get an exact price on WhatsApp?

You can contact the clinic for an initial discussion, but an exact treatment estimate generally requires an examination and appropriate imaging.

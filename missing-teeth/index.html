Why Replace Missing Teeth?

  • Improve chewing and everyday oral function.
  • Support speech and confidence where tooth loss affects them.
  • Help maintain a balanced bite when clinically appropriate.
  • Discuss replacement before adjacent teeth or remaining oral structures are affected by changes in function.

Which Option Is Right for You?

There is no single best replacement for everyone. The choice depends on the number and position of missing teeth, gum and bone health, bite, general dental condition, expectations and budget. A clinical examination and appropriate imaging help determine suitable options.

FAQs About Missing Teeth

Is a dental implant better than a bridge?

Not necessarily for every patient. Both can be appropriate in different situations. Your dentist can compare them based on oral health, adjacent teeth, bone support, time and budget.

Can missing teeth be replaced without implants?

Yes. Bridges and dentures can provide non-implant replacement options in suitable cases.

Can I replace several missing teeth?

Yes. Depending on the pattern of tooth loss, options may include multiple implants, implant-supported bridges, conventional bridges or dentures.

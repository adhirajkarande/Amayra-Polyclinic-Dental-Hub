WHAT AFFECTS THE FEE?

Why braces cost varies from one patient to another

Orthodontic treatment is planned around the patient's teeth, bite, growth or skeletal considerations, treatment goals and the appliance selected. Two people with similarly crowded teeth may still need different treatment plans.

Type of braces

Common options include conventional metal braces and more aesthetic ceramic or tooth-coloured appliances. The appliance choice can affect the fee.

Case complexity

Crowding, spacing, rotations, bite discrepancies and other alignment problems can require different levels of orthodontic planning.

Treatment duration

More involved cases may need a longer course of active treatment and more review appointments.

Age & treatment goals

Children, teenagers and adults can have different orthodontic considerations. The final plan depends on the individual examination.

Additional procedures

Some cases may need other dental treatment before, during or after orthodontics, such as treatment of cavities or gum problems.

Retention

Retainers help maintain the result after active tooth movement. Ask whether the retention phase and retainers are included in the quotation.

COMPARE THE OPTIONS

Metal braces, ceramic braces or clear aligners?

The right option depends on clinical suitability, treatment goals, lifestyle preferences and budget. Cost should be considered alongside predictability, maintenance and the type of tooth movement required.

Metal braces

A well-established fixed orthodontic option. They are visible but can be suitable for a wide range of alignment and bite problems.

Ceramic braces

Use more discreet tooth-coloured components. Suitability and cost depend on the appliance system and individual treatment plan.

Clear aligners

Removable transparent aligners may be appropriate for selected cases. See our Clear Aligners in Pune guide for more information.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

What should a braces quotation include?

  • Orthodontic consultation and treatment planning
  • Diagnostic records or imaging when indicated
  • The type of braces or appliance being used
  • Expected active-treatment visits and adjustments
  • Any additional dental procedures required
  • Retainers and the post-treatment retention plan
  • Whether the quoted amount is a package or has separate components

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about braces cost

How much do braces cost in Pune?

Fees vary according to the appliance, case complexity, expected duration, appointments and retention. An orthodontic assessment is needed for an individual estimate.

Are metal braces cheaper than ceramic braces?

They can have different fees because the appliance materials and systems differ. The overall treatment plan also affects the final cost.

Does braces cost include retainers?

Not necessarily. Retainers may be quoted separately, so ask what is included before starting treatment.

Does treatment duration affect the cost?

It can, particularly when a more complex case requires a longer active-treatment period and additional reviews.

Can I get an exact braces price online?

An exact estimate generally requires a clinical orthodontic assessment. Online guides explain cost factors but cannot provide a personalised quotation.
